# Reproducing the learned-routing campaign

These steps rebuild the campaign on a clean Linux x86_64 machine up to its current state: the
calibrated cells after calibration fix r0 (lr-cells-v5) and their frozen test set. Later stages
are marked **TBD** and show the commands the contract plans for them. Start with
[README.md](README.md) for what the campaign is.

**Off the original host, git plus the public sources are enough.** Every small file the steps
need is mirrored under `$NOTES/campaign/`: all six AgentX base manifests, both calibration driver
script sets, the two `cells_validation.json` files and the engine-config script
([section 17](#17-small-files-mirrored-from-the-cr)). Everything bulky (traces, replicates,
replay caches) is regenerated by the steps below.

**Path-redacted files.** This public copy replaces the campaign host's absolute paths with
placeholders such as `<campaign-root>` and `<worktree>`. In nine mirrored files that changes the
bytes, and so the SHA-256, but no value the harness reads. The campaign's records (the facts, the
audits, `facts/test_freeze.json` and the tables on this page) cite the SHA-256 of the original
files, which are not published:

What that means for a reproduction from this copy:

- `engine.json`, `SPLIT_MANIFEST.json` and `traces/MANIFEST.json`: the freeze check in step 11
  reports all three as mismatches. Nothing else depends on their bytes. A cell's content SHA-256
  hashes only the engine's `mock_engine_args` and `model`, so the published `engine.json` gives the
  same cell content SHA-256s as the original.
- The six AgentX base manifests: every generated AgentX trace hashes its base manifest's SHA-256
  into its header digest. Traces generated with the published manifests in place therefore differ
  from the frozen ones even when every row matches, so none of the 26 AgentX cells reproduces its
  `trace_sha256`, and the harness raises `CellError` on all of them. The per-play `base_sha256`
  check in step 8 still verifies the lowered rows. To evaluate AgentX cells anyway, re-pin their
  `trace_sha256` to your generated traces, which gives up the byte-identity check.
- Nothing else the steps check is affected: the source traces, and the 82 non-AgentX cells' traces
  (26 train, 10 val, 46 test) and content SHA-256s.

**Conventions:**

- `WT` is your checkout of branch `rupei/learned-routing` at the mirror snapshot commit (step 1).
- `NOTES` is `$WT/notes/learned-routing`.
- `LR_ROOT` is the campaign root, called `CR` in the contract and facts. The original is
  `<campaign-root>`.
- `PY` is `$WT/.venv/bin/python`.
- `CR/...` paths inside cells and facts are relative to `LR_ROOT`.
- "Verified" means the command or check was re-run while writing this page, on the campaign host
  against the original `CR` or, where stated, in a fresh private root. "Expected, not verified"
  marks claims that follow from the code and the facts but were not re-run. Everything else is
  taken from the facts files and the code.

**What reproduces exactly:**

- **Regenerated byte-identically by SHA-256, verified on the campaign host:**
  - the source traces (106/106 files, step 6);
  - the long session traces (seed 6 regenerated, step 6);
  - every trace the frozen cells reference: 34/34 train, 14/14 val and 60/60 test cells, including
    all 26 AgentX cells with the original base manifests in place (step 9). From this public copy,
    expect the 82 non-AgentX cells only ("Path-redacted files" above).
- **AgentX base rows** (`build-base`, per-play `base_sha256`): expected, not verified. The rows
  were not rebuilt while writing this page. The lowering is deterministic per the audit
  `agentx-lowering/parity-fidelity-r1`: an independent re-implementation of AISim's
  `weka.rs::lower_trace` reproduced the base rows of all 82 plays × 6 transforms field for field.
- **Candidate cells** (lr-cells-v3, `*.candidates.jsonl`): regenerated by step 7. Expected, not
  verified.
- **Frozen cells** (lr-cells-v5): **installed from git** (step 7), not regenerated. Their load
  levels follow from the mirrored load curves (verified: 171/171 levels per decision table, and
  108/108 cell loads equal their level, step 10). Re-running the calibration sweeps uses the
  mirrored scripts (step 10); that was not attempted off-host.
- **Generated AgentX traces:** byte-identical only when the cell's original base manifest is
  installed before generating. 21 AgentX cells use only `cap300`, and 5 use one other base each.
  Verified on the campaign host, where the originals are in place. The manifests in git are the
  path-redacted copies, not the originals, so from this public copy the generated AgentX traces
  don't match the frozen `trace_sha256` ("Path-redacted files" above).
- **Replays:** deterministic given the cell and the replicate k. The harness passes the policy
  seed k + 1 only to seeded policy types; the others take no seed (step 12). Bit-exact results
  across hosts are expected only on a matching OS and glibc image, because replay uses the host's
  libm (A9 addendum). Run the parity smoke at the end of step 9 before trusting results from
  another host.
- **Not carried over to another host:**
  - Result-cache keys. They include the bindings build ID: a SHA-256 over the JSON object
    `{core_so_sha256, aisimulate}`, that is, the `_core.abi3.so` SHA-256 together with the
    installed `aisimulate` version (`learned_routing/cache.py` `bindings_build_id`).
  - A few manifests that record absolute paths, and the path-redacted files above. See
    [Verify the frozen test set](#11-verify-the-frozen-test-set).

## 3. Bindings with `ais-forward-pass`

AIS engine timing exists only in bindings built with this feature. The feature name in this tree
is `ais-forward-pass` (`lib/bindings/python/Cargo.toml`). The `aic-forward-pass` name in older
skill text is stale (DEVIATIONS.md, setup). Run the exact command from `facts/build_rust.json` with
the venv's `maturin` on `PATH`, then install the Python packages in the order AGENTS.md uses:

```bash
cd $WT/lib/bindings/python && VIRTUAL_ENV=$WT/.venv PATH=$WT/.venv/bin:$PATH \
    maturin develop --uv --release --features ais-forward-pass
cd $WT
uv pip install --python $PY -e lib/gpu_memory_service
uv pip install --python $PY -e .          # pulls aisimulate==0.13.0.dev202609300000000061
```

An incremental release rebuild took about 2 minutes on the campaign host (`facts/build_rust.json`).

**Checks.** Both were verified:

```bash
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 $PY - <<'EOF'
from aisimulate_core.sdk import ForwardPassPerfModelConfig
from dynamo._internal.ais import estimate_canonical_num_gpu_blocks
c = ForwardPassPerfModelConfig(model="Qwen/Qwen3-32B", system="h100_sxm", backend="vllm",
                               backend_version="0.24.0", worker_type="aggregated", tp=2).to_dict()

                                        max_num_seqs=1024))   # expect 18863
```

To prove AIS timing is in effect, replay one 1,024-token request on one worker. TTFT must equal
the setup's `55.486801` ms; polynomial timing gives 32.49 ms (`facts/setup.json`). This is a single
smoke replay, outside the slot pool:

```bash
cat > /tmp/ais_smoke.py <<'EOF'
import json, sys, tempfile
from dynamo.mocker import MockEngineArgs
from dynamo.replay import run_trace_replay
engine = json.load(open(sys.argv[1]))
with tempfile.NamedTemporaryFile("w", suffix=".jsonl", delete=False) as f:
    f.write(json.dumps({"timestamp": 0, "input_length": 1024, "output_length": 1,
                        "hash_ids": [1, 2]}) + "\n")
report = run_trace_replay(
    f.name, extra_engine_args=MockEngineArgs.from_json(json.dumps(engine["mock_engine_args"])),
    router_mode="round_robin", num_workers=1, trace_format="mooncake", trace_block_size=512,
    capture_per_request=True)
print("ttft_ms", report.per_request[0]["ttft_ms"])
EOF
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 DYN_LOG=warn $PY /tmp/ais_smoke.py $NOTES/campaign/config/engine.json
```

**Build IDs.** The campaign's current bindings have `.so` SHA-256 `1d67c131…` and build ID
`6955b0ee…` (`facts/build_rust.json` `correction_fix_r0`; `facts/test_freeze.json`
`bindings_build_id`). A rebuild almost certainly yields another build ID: the `.so` embeds
build-tree RPATHs (UPSTREAM_FOLLOWUPS #7). A different build ID makes every cached result from the
original `CR` unreachable, which is intended. Use `lr-report --build-id PREFIX` to keep one build's
records.

**AgentX play selection** (`acquire.acquire_agentx`):

- A play is kept whole and unchanged iff every normal or streaming request, recursively including
  subagents, satisfies `in + max(out, 1) ≤ 131072`.
- Result: 82 of 393 plays, 3,132 requests, 39 explicit subagent groups and 59 single-model plays.
  No clipping or scaling, and no 32K fallback (`MANIFEST.json` `agentx.selection`).
- Each play is written to `CR/traces/agentx/plays/<row>-<id>.json`. Its SHA-256 equals the SHA-256
  of its source line.
- The plays are dealt into subsets A1–A3, V1–V3 and T1–T4 by a seeded round-robin with subagent
  plays first (`agentx-split-v1`). The subsets are in `SPLIT_MANIFEST.json`
  `agentx_play_subsets`.

**Check every trace file.** Verified 106/106 on the campaign host:

```bash
$PY - <<'EOF'
import hashlib, json, os
from pathlib import Path
cr, notes = Path(os.environ["LR_ROOT"]), Path(os.environ["NOTES"])
files = json.loads((notes / "campaign/traces/MANIFEST.json").read_text())["files"]
def sha(p):
    h = hashlib.sha256()
    with open(p, "rb") as f:
        for chunk in iter(lambda: f.read(1 << 22), b""):
            h.update(chunk)
    return h.hexdigest()
bad = [f["path"] for f in files if sha(cr / f["path"]) != f["sha256"]]
print(f"{len(files) - len(bad)}/{len(files)} trace files match", *bad, sep="\n")
EOF
```

## 9. Transforms and the calibrated traces

`learned_routing.workloads.transform` materializes `CR/traces/derived/<sha256>.jsonl` and
`<sha256>.meta.json`, named by content. The transforms are deterministic and seeded, and they run
in this order:

1. **Window** `[t0, t1)` ms (sessions whose first turn arrives in it, rebased), or `plays` for Weka.
2. **`prefix_root_mult`** k: disjoint hash-space copies of the root segments, keyed by the first
   block after each root.
3. **`isl_prefix_mult` / `isl_unique_mult`:** shared blocks are expanded consistently by hash; each
   request's unique suffix is scaled.
4. **`osl_mult`:** `max(1, round(out × m))`.
5. **`think_cap_s`, then `think_mult`:** delays for Mooncake sessions, time dilation for Weka plays.
6. **Context cap** at `max_model_len`.
7. **Dense hash renumbering.**

The full rules are in the module docstring (`learned_routing/workloads/transform.py`):

```bash
$PY -m learned_routing.workloads.transform --campaign-root $LR_ROOT \
    --source $LR_ROOT/traces/mooncake/mooncake_trace.jsonl --format mooncake --block-size 512 \
    --spec '{"window": [1800000.0, 2400000.0], "isl_unique_mult": 2.5, "seed": 0}'
```

Train ranges are `isl_*_mult` [1, 1.5], `osl_mult` [0.5, 2], `prefix_root_mult` [1, 2] and
`think_mult` [0.5, 2]. Test transform-extrapolation cells go beyond them (`SPLIT_MANIFEST.json`
`train_ranges`).

**Rebuild every trace the frozen cells reference.** Each trace is a pure function of the cell plus
the calibration rules (`facts/calibration.json` `rules.windows`):

- **Mooncake, FAST25 conversation, FAST25 synthetic:** the cell's `transform` applied to its
  source, block 512. These are the B2 candidate traces, unchanged by calibration.
- **Sessions:** the long seed trace from step 6, block 64.
  - Open loop: window `[0, (W + 540 + 60) s × speedup]` and `think_mult` = the cell's
    `think_mult` × speedup. Arrivals are pre-dilated, so think time doesn't depend on load or N.
    W is the cell's `measure.warmup_ms`: two session-lifetime envelopes from calibration fix r0,
    960 s at think 0.25 and 0.5, 1,200 s at think 1 (including islu1.5 and root2), 1,320 s at
    think 1.5 and 2,160 s at think 4.0. Each cell also records the result as
    `measure_trace.trace_window_ms` and `measure_trace.think_mult_in_trace`.
  - Closed loop: window `[0, (max(2C, 60) + 10C) s]` with concurrency C (the generator emits one
    session per second). The first max(2C, 60) sessions are an identity warm-up
    (`measure.warmup_trace_ms`). Every frozen closed-loop session cell has C ≥ 81, so this is 2C
    in practice.
  - These constants come from the fix r0 calibration driver (`calib.py`: `SESS_*`,
    `session_warmup_s`, `CLOSED_*`); see step 10.
- **AgentX:** `GenSpec(**cell["lowered"]["spec"])`.

The script below was verified on the campaign host: train 34/34, val 14/14 and test 60/60 cells
reproduced their `trace_sha256`. That covers every non-AgentX cell and all 26 AgentX cells (the
5 transformed ones included), with the original base manifests in place. From this public copy,
expect `ok` for the 82 non-AgentX cells (26 train, 10 val, 46 test) and `MISMATCH` for the 26
AgentX cells, whose base manifests are path-redacted (step 8); pass `--skip-agentx` to rebuild
only the others.

```bash
cat > /tmp/regen_cell_traces.py <<'EOF'
"""Rebuild every trace a frozen cells file references; check each against the cell's trace_sha256.
usage: regen_cell_traces.py ROOT CELLS.jsonl OUT_DIR [--skip-agentx]"""
import json, sys
from pathlib import Path
from learned_routing.workloads import agentx_lowered as ax
from learned_routing.workloads.transform import TransformSpec, materialize

root, cells_path, out_dir = Path(sys.argv[1]), Path(sys.argv[2]), Path(sys.argv[3])
skip_agentx = "--skip-agentx" in sys.argv[4:]
SOURCES = {"mooncake": "traces/mooncake/mooncake_trace.jsonl",
           "fast25_conversation": "traces/fast25/conversation_trace.jsonl",
           "fast25_synthetic": "traces/fast25/synthetic_trace.jsonl"}
WINDOW_S, TAIL_S = 540.0, 60.0  # open-loop sessions: replay-time window and arrival tail

def session_spec(cell):
    t, load = cell["transform"], cell["load"]
    think = float(t.get("think_mult", 1.0))
    if load["mode"] == "open_speedup":
        # warm-up W is in the cell (960-2160 s by transform); trace time = replay time x speedup
        warmup_s = cell["measure"]["warmup_ms"] / 1000.0
        window_ms = (warmup_s + WINDOW_S + TAIL_S) * 1000.0 * load["value"]
        think *= load["value"]
    else:
        c = load["value"]  # closed loop: max(2C, 60) identity warm-up sessions, then 10C measured
        window_ms = (max(2 * c, 60) + 10 * c) * 1000.0
    return {"window": [0.0, float(window_ms)], "isl_unique_mult": t.get("isl_unique_mult", 1.0),
            "isl_prefix_mult": t.get("isl_prefix_mult", 1.0), "osl_mult": t.get("osl_mult", 1.0),
            "prefix_root_mult": t.get("prefix_root_mult", 1), "think_mult": float(think),
            "seed": t.get("seed", 0), "max_model_len": t.get("max_model_len", 131072)}

bad = 0
for line in cells_path.read_text().splitlines():
    cell = json.loads(line)
    want = cell["trace_sha256"][0]
    if cell["family"] == "agentx":
        if skip_agentx:
            continue
        spec = dict(cell["lowered"]["spec"], plays=tuple(cell["lowered"]["spec"]["plays"]))
        got = ax.write_generated(ax.GenSpec(**spec), root, out_dir / f"{want}.jsonl")["sha256"]
    elif cell["family"] == "synthetic_sessions":
        seed = int(cell["segment"].split(":s")[1])
        source = root / f"traces/synthetic_sessions/long/sessions_seed{seed}_d48000.jsonl"
        got = materialize(source, "mooncake", TransformSpec.from_dict(session_spec(cell)),
                          cell["trace_block_size"], out_dir).sha256
    else:
        got = materialize(root / SOURCES[cell["family"]], "mooncake",
                          TransformSpec.from_dict(cell["transform"]), cell["trace_block_size"],
                          out_dir).sha256
    bad += got != want
    print("ok      " if got == want else "MISMATCH", cell["cell_id"], got[:16], flush=True)
sys.exit(1 if bad else 0)
EOF
mkdir -p $LR_ROOT/traces/derived $LR_ROOT/traces/agentx_lowered/gen
for s in train val test; do
  $PY /tmp/regen_cell_traces.py $LR_ROOT $LR_ROOT/cells/$s.jsonl $LR_ROOT/traces/derived
done
# AgentX outputs (<sha>.jsonl plus <sha>.jsonl.meta.json) belong in traces/agentx_lowered/gen
for meta in $LR_ROOT/traces/derived/*.jsonl.meta.json; do
  mv "${meta%.meta.json}" "$meta" $LR_ROOT/traces/agentx_lowered/gen/
done
```

**Levels from the mirrored curves.** Every level in a decision table is where default's mean good
fraction (`gf_default` in the `curves.json` rows) first crosses 0.95, 0.85 and 0.65, going up in
load. Between the two bracketing grid points, the good fraction is interpolated linearly against
log(load) (`analyze.py` `interp_level`), and the level is rounded to 6 significant digits. The
curve rows are already scored at the family's final (I, S). The script below re-derives every
level, base and transform, from the curves alone. Verified: 171/171 levels for both
`runs/calibrate/` and `runs/calibrate-fix-r0/`. Separately, every frozen cell's `load.per_worker`
equals its level in `runs/calibrate-fix-r0/decisions.json` (108/108).

```bash
cat > /tmp/levels_from_curves.py <<'EOF'
import json, math, sys
from pathlib import Path
TARGETS = {"L1": 0.95, "L2": 0.85, "L3": 0.65}
def level(rows, target):
    pts = sorted((r["load"], r["gf_default"]) for r in rows)
    for (x0, y0), (x1, y1) in zip(pts, pts[1:]):
        if y0 >= target > y1 or y0 > target >= y1:
            if y0 == y1:
                return x0
            f = (y0 - target) / (y0 - y1)
            return math.exp(math.log(x0) + f * (math.log(x1) - math.log(x0)))
    return None
d = Path(sys.argv[1])
curves = json.loads((d / "curves.json").read_text())
dec = json.loads((d / "decisions.json").read_text())
ok = bad = 0
def cmp(key, rows, want):
    global ok, bad
    for lvl, t in TARGETS.items():
        got = level(rows, t)
        got = None if got is None else float(f"{got:.6g}")
        if got == want[lvl]:
            ok += 1
        else:
            bad += 1
            print("MISMATCH", key, lvl, got, want[lvl])
for fam, modes in curves.items():
    if fam == "transforms":
        continue
    for mode, block in modes.items():
        for n, entry in block["by_n"].items():
            cmp(f"{fam}|{mode}|{n}", entry["curve"], dec["levels"][fam][mode][n])
for key, entry in curves["transforms"].items():
    cmp(key, entry["curve"], dec["transform_levels"][key])
print(f"{ok}/{ok + bad} levels match")
EOF
$PY /tmp/levels_from_curves.py $NOTES/campaign/runs/calibrate-fix-r0   # 171/171 levels match
```

## 12. Noise and replicate protocol

From CONTRACT A1, `facts/noise.json` and `facts/calibration.json` `rules.replicates`.

**Replicates.** `--repeats K` evaluates CRN workload replicates k = 0..K−1, shared by every policy.

| Protocol | Applies to | Replicate k |
|---|---|---|
| `crn-order-v1` | cells without `arrival_spread_ms`, that is FAST25 synthetic (not quantized) | a seeded permutation of tied arrivals, seed = `int(sha256("crn-order-v1\|<trace sha>\|<k>")[:8]) >> 1` (`facts/noise.json`) |
| `crn-spread-v1` | cells with `arrival_spread_ms`: Mooncake 3053, FAST25 conversation 3000, sessions 1000 ms | each session's first arrival re-drawn uniformly inside its logging slot; without it, logged bursts arrive simultaneously at any speedup (UPSTREAM_FOLLOWUPS #8) |
| `crn-order-v1`, copy permutation | AgentX lowered | a permutation of the recycled copies |

Replicate traces are materialized under `CR/runs/replicates/`.

**Policy seeds.** The harness writes `parameters.seed` = k + 1 into the replay YAML only for
seeded policy types: `SEEDED_TYPES` in `learned_routing/policy.py` is `dynamo-default-cost-fn`,
`learned-choice` and `sticky-session`, and a spec can override it with `"seeded": true|false`.
Other types, and `round_robin`, get no seed (`policy_seed` is null in their records). A spec that
puts `seed` in `parameters` is rejected. The setup stage checked determinism:

- `round_robin`, `lmetric`, two-tier and seeded default replay per-request identically across
  re-runs;
- `chwbl`, `llm-d-precise-prefix`, `llm-d-optimized-baseline`, `ramjet` and `dualmap` change
  routing deterministically with their parameters;
- unseeded default is nondeterministic, which is why the branch added the seed.

Sources: STATE.md, the setup and `setup/ais-and-policy r0` entries.

**Minimum replicates:**

| Use | Replicates |
|---|---:|
| Validation, test and the pilot gate | 3 |
| Each CMA-ES evaluation | 2 |
| Noise calibration, per family and N | 8 |

**Noise.** The pooled sd of the paired per-replicate ratio r[c][k] = goodput(policy, c, k) /
goodput(default@defaults, c, k), computed by `learned_routing.noise`.

**Differs beyond noise.** Both clauses must hold, and a zero spread never passes
(`noise.differs_beyond_noise`):

1. Some validation cell has |mean_k r − 1| > 3 × the pooled sd.
2. Take the per-segment mean delta, then the mean of those over independent segments (at least 3).
   Its absolute value must exceed 2 × its SE: |mean| > 2 × SE. The test is two-sided in code
   (`abs(mean_delta) > k_se * se`), so a policy worse than default, such as round-robin, also
   passes.

**Measured:**

- Setup fix r0: goodput CV of 1.17–3.85% for every policy across 8 replicates on 3 Mooncake
  2,000-row cells (STATE.md, `fix(setup r0)` entry, 2026-10-02 13:59:24 PDT).
- Calibration fix r0: pooled paired-ratio sd of 0.0037–0.045 across the 29 family × N × mode
  groups (`facts/noise_calibration.json`).
- Round-robin differs from default beyond noise on both train (11 segments; mean segment delta
  −0.462, SE 0.062) and val (6 segments; −0.474, SE 0.065).
- The 11 train segments are the 10 of the split design plus `agentx:A1+A2`, the segment label of
  the pooled cell `agentx-A1_A2-base-n8-lanes-L3`. That cell draws plays from both A1 and A2, so
  it overlaps those two segments, but the noise rule counts it as a separate segment.
  `SPLIT_MANIFEST.json` `segments_by_split` lists only the 10.

## 13. Running `lr-eval`, `lr-train` and `lr-report`

Every local replay takes one of 20 `flock` slots in `CR/slots/`. Run no replays outside the pool,
except single smoke runs. `lr-eval --slots-status` shows the holders.

Every long job is chunked: call it with `--max-wall-seconds 540` until it no longer exits 3.

| Exit code | `lr-eval` | `lr-train` |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | done | done |
| 1 | usage or setup error | error |
| 2 | some replays errored | — |
| 3 | stopped at the deadline; call again | paused; call again |

**Policy specs** (`learned_routing/policy.py`):

- A spec is one JSON or YAML object, or a JSONL of them.
- The builtin names `default` (= `default@defaults`, `dynamo-default-cost-fn` with no parameters)
  and `round_robin` need no file.
- Default-cost knobs that live in `KvRouterConfig` go in `router_config`.

```jsonl
{"name": "lmetric@defaults", "router_mode": "kv_router", "type": "lmetric", "parameters": {}}
{"name": "sticky-hard", "router_mode": "kv_router", "type": "sticky-session", "parameters": {"mode": "hard"}}
{"name": "lc-theta0-parity", "router_mode": "kv_router", "type": "learned-choice", "parameters": {"feature_set": "v1", "theta": [-1, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0], "tie_break": "reservoir"}}
```

`learned-choice` at θ = −e₀ with `tie_break: reservoir` reproduces the seeded default request for
request. Training uses the default `one_draw` (`facts/integration.json`). Save the example specs
as `specs.jsonl` for the commands below.

```bash
cd $WT/benchmarks/learned_routing
# evaluate (module docstring example); records append to --out, results are cached
while true; do
  lr-eval --policy-spec default round_robin specs.jsonl --cells $LR_ROOT/cells/val.jsonl \
      --out $LR_ROOT/runs/pilot/r1/results.jsonl --repeats 3 --max-wall-seconds 540; rc=$?
  [ "$rc" -eq 3 ] || break
done
# tune (resumable; refuses to resume if the space, cells, reference, objective or seeds changed)
lr-train --space spaces/learned_choice_m1.yaml --cells $LR_ROOT/cells/train.jsonl \
    --val $LR_ROOT/cells/val.jsonl --run-dir $LR_ROOT/runs/pilot/m1-s1 \
    --budget-evals 400 --popsize 16 --seed 1 --max-wall-seconds 540
# report: paired ratios against default@defaults, cluster bootstrap, tables, figures
lr-report --results $LR_ROOT/runs/pilot/r1/results.jsonl --out-dir $LR_ROOT/report/pilot \
    --metric goodput_rps_window --cluster segment --split val
```

## 14. Gate and headline test

**Pilot gate** (CONTRACT, Noise section). Escalate to phase 2 only if all three hold:

1. Fewer than 2% of replays error.
2. The projected full-run cost is feasible. Per A9 (section 13), any batch or tuning job
   projected at more than about 1 hour of local wall-clock goes to the CPU-cluster lane once
   `facts/remote.json` reports `ok`. This replaces the 8-hour threshold in the CONTRACT's Noise
   section.
3. Some policy differs from default beyond noise on validation (section 12), with ≥ 3 replicates.

If clause 3 fails, recalibrate the loads once and re-check. A second failure stops the campaign
with a diagnostic report.

**Headline test**, pre-registered in `facts/HEADLINE_TEST.json` before any training:

- **Comparison:** the learned model against the validation-selected best baseline, on the frozen
  test set (60 cells). Each policy is evaluated once with replicates k = 0, 1, 2, paired on the same
  replicate.
- **Per-cell statistic:** mean over replicates of
  clip(log(goodput_rps_window(learned) / goodput_rps_window(best)), −log 3, +log 3).
- **Per-segment statistic:** the mean of the per-cell statistic over the segment's cells. There are
  12 segments:
  - mooncake w3 and w5;
  - sessions s6–s9;
  - AgentX T1–T4;
  - FAST25 synthetic x0 and x1.

  FAST25 conversation cells count toward w3 and w5.
- **Primary test:** a one-sided exact Wilcoxon signed-rank test over the 12 segment statistics, at
  α = 0.05.
- **Effect size:** P(learned > best) with a 95% segment-cluster bootstrap CI. It is significant if
  the lower bound is > 0.5, and meaningful if the upper bound is > 0.75.
- **Stratification:**
  - by load mode;
  - per N, with Holm correction for per-N claims;
  - the transform-extrapolation stratum;
  - per family, descriptive only.
- **Parity:** TOST margin ±0.5% normalized goodput. MDE = max(1%, 2 × the validation SE).
- **Reported, not tested:**
  - SLO-scale rescoring at {0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2, 3} × (I, S);
  - router-state lag {0, 50, 200} ms (A5);
  - `speedup_ratio` and `decode_speedup_ratio` at 0.8 and 1.2.
