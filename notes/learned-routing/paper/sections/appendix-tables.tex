\section{Full result tables}
\label{app:tables}

This appendix holds the full result tables that \cref{sec:results} shows in part.
\Cref{tab:leaderboard-full} gives every test policy. For selected policies,
\cref{tab:N-ramjet,tab:N-default} break the result down by worker count,
\cref{tab:family-ramjet,tab:family-default} by family, \cref{tab:mode} by load mode and
\cref{tab:holdout} by hold-out stratum. \Cref{tab:coef-v1} gives the coefficients of the
\code{v1} learned arms, \cref{tab:secondary-full,tab:secondary-full-b} the secondary metrics of
\cref{sec:gaming}, and \cref{fig:profiles} the performance profiles. Intervals are 95\%
segment-bootstrap intervals ($\dagger$: within-segment cell bootstrap for strata with fewer than
three segments); only the headline row of \cref{tab:leaderboard-full} is a test.
