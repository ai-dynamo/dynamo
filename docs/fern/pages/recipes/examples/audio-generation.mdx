Generate speech with `Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-CustomVoice`. See
[Text-to-Audio](../../use-cases/diffusion/workflows/text-to-audio.md) for requests, or
[Audio and Realtime Serving](audio.mdx) for transcription and speech-to-speech.

`agg_omni_audio.yaml` was functionally validated on October 12, 2026 with one H100 GPU and Dynamo
1.5.1 serving images and operator; it is a correctness check, not a performance benchmark. For
`response_format: "wav"` with `data_source: "b64_json"`, save the raw response bytes as a WAV; the
response is not a JSON/base64 envelope.

<Warning>
**Experimental.** Prefer `agg_omni_audio.yaml` on Dynamo 1.5.1 or later. This earlier configuration
was validated on September 18, 2026 with one H100 GPU, the Dynamo 1.4.2 operator, and the pinned
`20260918-a9792db` frontend/worker pair: Dynamo `1.6.0.dev20260918`, vLLM `0.29.0`, and vLLM-Omni
`0.29.0rc1`. Keep that pair together and `runtimeVersionOverride: "1.6.0"` on both components; it
describes the serving images, not the operator. Replace `model-cache-pvc` and `subPath: model-cache`
with your writable cache and set your GPU node selectors. Namespace/node-pool bindings and cache names
were generalized from the tested demo; revalidate after adapting them. The test checked non-silent
waveform delivery and WAV framing, not listening quality or performance.
</Warning>
