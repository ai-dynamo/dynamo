Multimodal serving puts a vision encoder in front of the usual prefill and decode stages. When all three share the same GPUs, image processing stalls token generation. Encode-prefill-decode (EPD) disaggregation moves the encoder onto its own workers so each stage scales independently — but it also adds an embedding transfer, so it is not a free win. Our blog post, [When to Use Encode-Prefill-Decode Disaggregation to Accelerate Multimodal Model Serving](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/when-to-use-encode-prefill-decode-disaggregation-to-accelerate-multimodal-model-serving/), is a guide to the conditions under which disaggregating the encoder actually pays off.

## When disaggregation helps

EPD yields the most benefit when encoder work is a substantial share of request latency:

- **Heavy media input.** Multiple images, high-resolution images, or video generate more visual tokens and therefore more encoder work — giving disaggregation more to offload. EPD also lets you scale encoder capacity independently to prevent encoding from becoming a bottleneck.
- **Short to moderate output lengths.** Shorter output sequences keep decode from overshadowing encoder gains, so end-to-end improvements are clearest here.
- **Smaller, MoE, and quantized models.** These have a higher encoder-to-LLM compute ratio, making the encoder a larger relative bottleneck and EPD a bigger lever.
- **Mixed text and multimodal traffic.** Disaggregation isolates encoder work so that latency-sensitive text requests are not held up waiting for image encoding to complete.

## When it does not

EPD is less effective when decoder compute dominates:

- **Long output sequences.** As output length grows, decode increasingly determines total latency and the encoder's contribution shrinks to the point where disaggregation overhead can outweigh its gains.
- **Large dense models.** A bigger LLM means the encoder is a smaller fraction of overall compute, so isolating it moves less of the critical path. In these cases, scaling decoder capacity will have more impact than disaggregating the encoder.
